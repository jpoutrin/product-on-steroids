Edge: delivering bad news to a conflict-averse leader who hates surprises. The difficulty is the
dual risk — the CTO could hear it from the partner first AND tends to shoot the messenger. The skill
must not produce a softened, hedge-everything brief. It must produce one that leads with transparency,
sequences stakeholder notification deliberately, and handles the "why didn't I know" objection
directly rather than pivoting away from it.
