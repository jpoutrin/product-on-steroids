Happy path: a clear meeting purpose with a known leader profile and historical context. Guards against
generic prep advice that ignores the leader's specific lens (data-driven, launch-focused) and fails to
address the awkward history of a prior failed ask. The skill must surface that uncomfortable context
rather than pretend it doesn't exist.
