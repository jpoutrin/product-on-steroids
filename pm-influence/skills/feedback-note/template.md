# Feedback Note — <Recipient Name>, <Date>

<!--
Direction: [Positive | Constructive]
Relationship: [Direct report | Peer | Cross-functional partner]
-->

## Opening
<One sentence naming the recipient and the purpose of this note — e.g.,
"I wanted to share some feedback on [specific event/period]." Keep it warm
and direct; avoid preamble.>

## Situation
<When and where this happened. Anchor in a specific event, meeting, project
phase, or date range. 1–2 sentences. No opinions or judgments here — only
observable context.>

## Behavior
<What the recipient did — the observable action. Use second person ("you
proposed / you said / you chose to"). Avoid interpretation, trait labels
(e.g., "you were careless"), or hedging ("you seemed to"). 1–3 sentences.>

## Impact
<The concrete effect of that behavior on the team, project, stakeholder, or
outcome. Name who or what was affected and how. For constructive notes, state
the negative effect plainly without exaggeration. For positive notes, name what
was made better or enabled. 1–3 sentences.>

## Close
<Optional but recommended. For positive: one sentence reinforcing the behavior
and inviting repetition. For constructive: one sentence stating the specific
change you are asking for.>
