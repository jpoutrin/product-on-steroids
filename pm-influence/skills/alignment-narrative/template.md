# Alignment Narrative: <Topic / Initiative>

**Audience:** <role(s) — e.g., VP Engineering, CFO, cross-functional leads>
**Author:** <PM name>
**Date:** <YYYY-MM-DD>

---

## Situation
<2–4 sentences of uncontested, shared reality. Facts the audience already
accepts — no ask, no editorial. Anchor in the most recent shared data point
or agreed-upon context.>

## Complication
<3–6 sentences identifying the single sharpest tension, disruption, or gap
that makes the Situation insufficient. Cite specific evidence: a metric shift,
competitive move, customer behavior change, or constraint newly visible. Avoid
vague urgency ("customers want X") — name dates, numbers, or named events.>

## The Question
<One sentence stating the central question the Complication forces. Begin with
"So the question is…" or "This raises the question…" It must feel inevitable,
not manufactured. A skeptic should agree this is the right question.>

## Strategic Direction
<4–8 sentences committing to one answer. State the proposed direction clearly,
then give 2–3 reasons ordered by the audience's priorities (not the PM's). Do
not present options here — the narrative commits to a direction.>

## Call to Action
<One sentence. A concrete, time-bound request: a decision to be made, an
unblock to grant, an endorsement to give. Name the forum and the deadline if
possible — e.g., "We are asking for your endorsement at the Q3 planning
meeting on [date] to proceed with [action]."
>
