This is the canonical happy-path scenario: a clear new-product concept with well-defined target customer and problem space.
The skill should generate 3–4 focused, distinct experiments, isolate the riskiest assumptions (demand, problem fit, willingness to pay),
and specify metrics and thresholds that will drive a go/no-go decision. Guards against vague outputs that list methods without tying them to assumptions.
