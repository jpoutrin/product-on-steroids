## Test Questions

1. **Comprehension:** <A question that checks whether the target customer immediately understands what the product does and who it is for — e.g., "In your own words, what problem does this solve for you?">

2. **Differentiation:** <A question that checks whether the statement separates the product from alternatives in the customer's mind — e.g., "How is this different from [named competitor or current approach]?">

3. **Purchase relevance:** <A question that checks whether the positioning motivates action — e.g., "Based on this description, how urgent would it be for you to evaluate this?">
